## Implemented behavior

- `features/navigation/session.ts`: session-owned foreground watcher, optional reliable compass watcher, generation guards, abortable profile-preserving reroute, pause/resume, Stop/unmount cleanup. No route history or background service.
- `location.ts`: Expo High accuracy, minimum 3-second / 2-metre walking updates. Foreground permission and services check, bounded initial fix, native subscription callbacks. Unknown or >35 m accuracy and >15-second-old fixes cannot advance navigation.
- `geometry.ts`: local tangent-plane projection along real WGS84 geometry, along-distance and remaining path. Small drift hysteresis, implausible spatial jumps and ambiguous large along-path jumps near hairpins are filtered. ETA scales the backend walking-time estimate by remaining route geometry; it is not a personal walking-speed estimator.
- Off-route policy: >max(35 m, 1.5 x accuracy), at least 3 accepted updates spanning 6 seconds. At most one reroute every 20 seconds; API receives latest position, original destination, same profile and applicable preferences. Failed reroutes retain the route/profile, warn and offer Retry. Stale completions cannot revive a stopped session.
- Arrival: remaining mapped geometry <=20 m AND actual chosen destination <=20 m, accuracy <=20 m, at least two readings over 2 seconds. Arrival and Stop dispose both subscriptions and pending network work. Unmapped endpoint gaps remain explicit.
- Background pauses watchers/requests. Foreground resume rechecks permission/services/current position. Leaving Planner stops. Process/Expo reload and Android activity recreation end the in-memory session.
- Map-first preview, compact search, route alternatives, persistent Start, dedicated guidance/progress/Stop, GPS puck/accuracy/optional compass, remaining path and Recenter. Panning/zooming pauses follow locally before the bridge roundtrip. Leaflet remains north-up; native maps can use heading. Camera fit/follow accounts for overlay insets.
- No invented turn instructions: guidance explicitly says turn-by-turn is unavailable. GPS is never generated by the app. The GPX replay uses Android Emulator's location input and the ordinary Expo watcher.
